# Reviewed Excel updates and manual adjustments

The import center has Upload Excel, Manual adjustments and Publication history entries. Sales and AR share the center; the identified dataset determines review and replacement behavior. Upload never activates data. Employees with edit scope can submit and inspect requests. Editors cannot publish. Authorized region administrators may publish scoped source/AR updates; only Super Admin approves formal budgets and annual Sales/Prospect/P1/aggregate corrections, and restores publications. Account/password administration is separate and its permissions are unchanged by this release.

## Excel intake and review

One batch contains one supported dataset: weekly Sales, team actual Sales/budgets, customer monthly, complete DEHK/DDC aging, the explicitly recognized DDC Chinese ledger, or Unknown/Advance. Unknown, incomplete or mixed templates are rejected rather than partially imported. The DDC ledger replaces only DDC invoices; review names the Entity scope and preserves DEHK and shared receipts. Other aging templates still require both Entity sheets for full replacement. Critical formula errors or missing caches block publication. The parser does not execute macros, recalculate formulas or refresh links. Originals are archived by SHA-256 in private persistent storage, outside public Git.

Weekly defaults select changed latest weeks and new weeks for each Entity/fiscal year; historical revisions and gaps require explicit selection. First import selects all valid supplied scopes. All available sheets may support monthly derivation, without being selected for replacement. Same Entity/year published history can fill only missing derivations and records its evidence. Unchanged ranges are not republished. Customer months, team actual sources, aging and payments remain independent. Full AR replacement requires both Entity sheets, valid reporting dates and nonempty valid Entity records; a partial workbook cannot clear the other Entity.

Review binds the batch, selected scopes, source versions, approved budget/correction versions and any reviewed team reference. It displays raw and reporting amounts, sales-type comparisons, monthly/annual reconciliation and record counts. Source errors block publication; Review findings require written confirmation. Refresh is required after conflicting changes. Scope and current actor permissions are rechecked inside the database transaction. Same-batch publish retries are idempotent.

Team Prospect opt-in is presented only while reviewing a weekly Sales batch. It updates exactly the selected Entity/year/week scopes in the pinned team reference. Future imports do not implicitly advance it. Sales Team2 mapping and actual Sales/budgets remain independent.

## Manual adjustments

No workbook is required. An employee or administrator selects Entity, fiscal year, exact week, metric, sales type, replacement value and reason, then submits a review request. Supported annual corrections are YTD Sales, Prospect/source orderbook, additional P1 and annual Sales + Prospect; formal Annual Budget supports total and optional reconciled External/Group splits. No monthly/team/customer allocation is invented. Corrections expire outside their specified weeks; source Excel amounts remain unchanged. Budget approval versions survive subsequent weekly uploads. Team actuals and team budgets continue to come from their independent reviewed source; Entity corrections do not fabricate team allocations.

AR adjustments search only editable source invoices/receipts. One request replaces one record's explicitly selected fields: invoice amount, invoice/due/reporting date, aging, customer, Sales Team or PIC; receipts support balance, received date, customer, team or PIC. Currency, Entity, category and original file/sheet/row cannot be changed. Negative and zero balances are valid. Due/reporting-date edits recalculate overdue days and aging against the source reporting date, never today's date. Amount-only edits preserve other source fields. Source aging evidence stays available. Shared Unknown/Advance requires both DEHK and DDC edit scopes. A record changed since the request was prepared requires a new request; refreshing does not silently overwrite concurrent edits.

The manual form initially selects the latest published Entity/year/week inside the user's edit scope, rather than a fixed calendar week. Every requested correction week must have a matching published source. Clearing a due/reporting date clears derived overdue days and aging to unknown unless a replacement aging bucket is explicitly supplied; original source aging remains evidence.

## Publication history and restoration

Each new publication stores selected ranges, before/after values, source fingerprint/template, publisher, explanation and revision. Restoring is a separate Super Admin preview/confirmation. It changes only the saved ranges; accounts, unrelated Entities/periods and independent datasets remain untouched. Team-reference changes made by that publication are restored together, using scoped before-images. Later overlapping publications or mismatched after-images block direct restoration. A full AR replacement overlaps its individual record adjustments. Older batches without before-images have no restore button. Application history supplements database backups; it is not immutable off-server audit storage.

Reporting week selection and View previous week are read-only: they cap source snapshots/order lines to the chosen week and affect KPIs, applicable charts and data/image exports. Each Entity uses its latest available source at or before that week, with mixed source weeks explicit. Later monthly team actuals are unavailable in earlier views rather than copied backward. Customer-month and AR sources retain their independent dates. This does not delete or reactivate any publication. Source restoration and historical viewing are distinct operations.

AR restoration checks overlap by data kind and Entity, including full versus scoped replacement. An unrelated Entity's aging update or a separate payment publication does not block an invoice-only restoration. A later full aging publication that includes the same Entity does block it, even when that Entity's amounts were unchanged in that later publication.

## Release verification

Use isolated fixtures for publication, adjustment, restoration and account boundary tests. Verify actual financial sources remain unchanged across existing region/year/type/team combinations before deployment. Check new/latest/history defaults, repeated uploads, stale review, formula caches, fiscal years, partial AR rejection, manual negative/zero values, scoped restoration including team reference, India/partial-Entity API access, bilingual mobile UI and matching exports. Production release health checks remain read-only. Back up the persistent state before rollout; no acceptance test changes real business amounts.
