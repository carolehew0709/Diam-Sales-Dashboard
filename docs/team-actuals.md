# Team actual Sales and source team budgets

Owner confirmation, 2026-11-05: the separately supplied monthly team workbook provides actual Sales in EUR. Use its `Sheet1` SALES TEAM1 -> SALES TEAM2 mapping for the top team selector and team reporting; add actual monthly/cumulative charts and team annual budgets/attainment. `lib/team-actual-parser.ts` recognizes the mapping headers, reads the visible `Sales Forecast` detail, and reconciles each mapped team's Jan-through-source-month actuals/YTD to Sheet1 and its grand total. Formula errors, missing formula caches, unknown Entity/type/team, shifted headers and reconciliation failures block parsing. The hidden `Sales Forecast (2)` and future mixed Order book+FCST columns are reference evidence, not imported amounts. Detail and subtotals are never added together. In this supported template an empty actual detail cell is an unallocated/zero month; future actual months remain null. Budget allocations retain null on raw rows and the assigned source budget is summed without inferring missing allocations or monthly budget phasing.

All amounts are divided by 1,000 once into kEUR. `Store.teamActualData` is a separate monthly source, activated only through analyze -> review -> explicit publish in the existing database transaction. A publication replaces that source and mapping only; ordinary weekly imports do not overwrite it. Original team names, Bill-to evidence, row numbers, source name/sheet, periods, unit and raw allocation status are retained. Source and private publication backups are excluded from Git.

Reporting sums only authorized Entity rows and the chosen sales type/team. FY2026 DDC Group stays excluded. Only DEHK/DDC have actual allocations in this source, so the module does not claim full China/APAC coverage. The earlier LVMH+Exclusive Team reporting label is mapped to the supplied LVMH Team/Panny label; raw weekly rows stay unchanged. Unassigned old Prospect rows remain Unassigned rather than guessed.
